# Hi, I'm Derek!

I'm deeply interested in how machines learn to make decisions, understand speech, and interact with the world around them. I'm also a CS major at Georgia Tech, studying Artificial Intelligence and Embedded Devices.

I like figuring things out by building them, from learning agents to the geometry behind a rendered image.

A few of my own projects:

[**3D Raytracer from Scratch**](https://github.com/d-kwong/3D-Raytracer-from-scratch): I wrote rendering experiments to understand ray–triangle intersections, camera rotations, and lighting, then split pixel work across CPU threads.

<a href="https://github.com/d-kwong/3D-Raytracer-from-scratch"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/d-kwong/3D-Raytracer-from-scratch/main/docs/images/swirl-cover.png" width="320" alt="A swirl surface rendered with flat triangle shading"></a>

[**Recurrent Racer**](https://github.com/d-kwong/recurrent-racer): I built a Unity racing environment and a reinforcement learning agent that learns to steer and control the throttle on generated tracks.

<a href="https://github.com/d-kwong/recurrent-racer"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/d-kwong/recurrent-racer/main/docs/media/compact/50007-chase.gif" width="320" alt="A trained racing agent driving a generated track"></a>

[**macOS TodoList**](https://github.com/d-kwong/macOS-TodoList): I built a SwiftUI Kanban board to explore custom drag-and-drop interactions and saving tasks locally.

At Georgia Tech, I'm also working with teams:

- **Graption, with Big Data Big Impact:** I'm developing speech tone and emotion classifiers alongside an on-device pipeline for speech segmentation and transcription.
- **RoboCup, with RoboJackets:** I contribute to robotic soccer software and am exploring reinforcement learning for more adaptive gameplay decisions.

Previously, I worked on biologically inspired speech recognition with spiking neural networks at Tufts' Allen Discovery Center.

Outside of coding, I play alto and tenor saxophone and enjoy rock climbing.
